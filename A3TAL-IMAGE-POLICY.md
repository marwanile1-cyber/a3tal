# A3tal Editorial Image Policy

This policy is mandatory for every new or updated editorial image on **a3tal.com**.

## Core rule

Images must look like **real automotive photography**, not illustrations.

Use photorealistic, scene-based, dynamic images that could plausibly have been photographed in a real workshop, garage, road test, service bay, parts store, showroom, or diagnostic session.

## Required visual style

- Photorealistic cars, motorcycles, engines, suspension parts, tools and workshop environments.
- Human activity whenever it improves the story: technician inspecting, measuring, scanning, repairing, testing or comparing.
- Realistic lighting, reflections, depth of field, materials, dirt, grease and workshop context when appropriate.
- Article-specific scenes. The image must visually explain or support the exact topic.
- Named vehicles or motorcycles must match the actual model/body/design closely. Do not substitute a generic vehicle.
- Fault-code articles should show a realistic diagnostic situation: real vehicle, OBD scanner, multimeter, sensor, wiring or relevant component.
- Suspension/mechanical articles should show the actual component installed or being inspected in a realistic vehicle/workshop context.
- Featured images should normally be 1600x900 WebP and suitable for Google Discover.
- Internal images should also be high-resolution WebP and visually different from the featured image.
- A small A3tal.com brand mark is allowed when useful, but it must not dominate the scene.

## Forbidden by default

Do not publish any of the following unless the article specifically requires a technical diagram:

- Flat vector illustrations.
- Cartoon or comic imagery.
- Large icon-based compositions.
- Abstract infographic-style drawings.
- Fake dashboard-style graphics used as the main image.
- Generic AI-looking cars that do not match the named model.
- 3D-looking mechanical parts floating on dark backgrounds.
- Text-heavy banners or promotional copy on featured images.
- Reusing the same base image across multiple articles.

## Quality check before publish

Before setting an image as featured or inserting it in an article, verify:

1. It matches the exact article subject.
2. It looks photographic at first glance.
3. Vehicle/model details are correct when a specific model is named.
4. The scene is believable and technically relevant.
5. It is not a vector, diagram, icon composition or generic render.
6. Featured image and og:image are the same intended image.
7. The image is large enough for Google Discover and max-image-preview:large.
8. It is original to A3tal.com and not reused from another article.

If any check fails, the image is rejected and must be regenerated before publication.
